# References

## Models
- G. Jocher, J. Qiu. *Ultralytics YOLO11*, 2024. https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics (AGPL-3.0). Pre-labelling detector.
- A. Kirillov et al. *Segment Anything.* ICCV 2023. arXiv:2304.02643.
- C. Zhang et al. *Faster Segment Anything: Towards Lightweight SAM for Mobile Applications* (MobileSAM). arXiv:2306.14289, 2023. Default CPU segmenter.
- N. Ravi et al. *SAM 2: Segment Anything in Images and Videos.* arXiv:2408.00714, 2024. GPU segmenter option (SAM 2.1).

## Datasets
- Ultralytics. *African Wildlife dataset* (buffalo, elephant, rhino, zebra). https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/detect/african-wildlife/
- T.-Y. Lin et al. *Microsoft COCO: Common Objects in Context.* ECCV 2014. arXiv:1405.0312. (Detector pre-training; COCO128-seg subset for mask evaluation.)

## Evaluation methodology
- COCO detection evaluation, `pycocotools`. https://github.com/cocodataset/cocoapi
- K. Sofiiuk, I. Petrov, A. Konushin. *Reviving Iterative Training with Mask Guidance for Interactive Segmentation* (RITM, NoC metric and click simulation). ICIP 2022. arXiv:2102.06583.
- D. P. Papadopoulos et al. *Extreme Clicking for Efficient Object Annotation.* ICCV 2017. arXiv:1708.02750. (Cost of manual box drawing.)
